Scoring an hour by eye (the card)

  1. Load gap (Meteologica − IESO, Tesla when Meteologica is missing): +250 → +2, +100 → +1, −100 → −1, −250 → −2.
  2. Wind gap (IESO − Meteologica): +200 → +1, −200 → −1. Public spare under 2,500: double the load and wind points.
  3. Wind 3 h change: −300 or more (wind leaving) → +1, +300 (arriving) → −1. Wind upside: highest weather model 800+ MW above IESO wind → −1.
  4. DA vs usual: +18 → −2, +8 → −1, −8 → +1. NY − Ontario DA: ±$10 → ±1 (page 2).
  5. Card ±3 or more → strong. Then check page 2: same side as the read = tier A; read ±5 with a weaker card = tier B; card and read disagree = stand aside.
  6. Soft system: spare over 5,500 and wind over 2,000 → longs win about 1 hour in 3.

Read = expected RT − DA ($/MWh) with weights refit that morning on the prior 90 days through D−2. Card = the points on page 1. Tier A: card ±3 and read the same side. B: card under ±3, read ±5 or more. C: card and read disagree, stand aside. N: no tested edge.
