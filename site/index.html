Buy ladder — bids (buy DA)

Sell ladder — offers (sell DA)

A bid clears when DA settles at or below its price; an offer clears when DA settles at or above its price. Tiers add up: a bid ladder of 35 @ $58, 30 @ $50, 20 @ $45 holds 35 MW if DA settles at $55, 65 MW at $48 and 85 MW at $44. Bids are listed highest price first, offers lowest price first. Tick "MW as running total" if your IESO tool wants cumulative MW per point.

East–Ottawa pair

Sell East DA + buy Ottawa DA, same MW, same hour. Pays when Ottawa's RT premium over East beats its DA premium.
Pair legs go into the normal ladders as an extra first tier: an East offer at −$100 and an Ottawa bid at $2,000, so both clear at the DA price. Copy both hubs' ladders from the DA Virtual tab, or you hold only one leg. The legs share the zone limits with the ladder (East 85 MW per side, Ottawa 100), so the pair shrinks in hours where the ladder already uses the room.
