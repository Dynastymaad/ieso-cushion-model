How to use the columns

What could go wrong flags the three tested unpriced signs: Load = Tesla ≥ IESO + 150; Wind = IESO wind falling 200+ MW over 3 h; Gas ramp = gas need rising 500+ MW over 3 h. vs usual = our DA forecast minus its 30-day median for that hour (well above usual leans sell). Tight headroom, low spare and high CAHR raise spike odds but DA usually over-prices them (sells). Wind 3h = IESO wind now minus 3 hours earlier. Models low/high exclude any weather model frozen in the feed. Hours are hour-ending EST. Model call: SELL = tight/surplus sell, BUY = buy band, SW = Spike Watch long.
