Headroom (8,261 gas + 6,049 hydro available − 7,956) = 6,354. Under ~7,000 the model calls a tight hour and sells, because DA has historically over-priced those hours. The question each day is whether something DA can't see will push RT higher anyway.

Six questions for a read

  1. Is DA already pricing it? CAHR ≥ 12 means DA is already at scarcity prices. A sell there needs RT to stay calm, not just "not spike".
  2. Will load come in above IESO? Tesla − IESO ≥ +200 (green) is the most useful single sign. Tesla normally sits below IESO.
  3. Can wind disappoint? Compare IESO wind with the models' low. A big gap with wind falling into the evening is how Oct 1, 7 and 8 went wrong.
  4. How hard is the ramp? Ramp 3h in the top 5% (dark green) means gas has to come on fast. Spikes happen inside the hour when units lag.
  5. How much spare is left? Spare under 1,500 (amber) leaves little room for any surprise.
  6. What did the last week do? Last 7d RT − DA and x/7. A big average from 1–2 days (low x/7) is one spike, not a pattern.

Column guide

Load: IESO / Tesla / Dynasty / Meteo
Demand forecasts. DA leans on IESO's. Tesla − IESO is the one that matters most: positive is unusual and means load may come in above what DA priced.
Wind: IESO / Meteo / Models low–high
IESO's and Meteologica's wind, plus the lowest and highest of five weather models. A model low far under IESO means wind could disappoint. Amber when the low is under half of IESO.
Solar
Gone by HE18 in October. Its fall is part of the evening ramp.
Nuclear, Hydro exp
Nuclear is flat (7,539 all day). Hydro exp is the hydro we expect to run; it peaks in the evening to cover the ramp.
Net exports
Expected exports (+ = Ontario exporting). Exports add to what gas must cover; cuts after the bid help RT, adds hurt.
Load net of nuc/wind/solar
What hydro, gas and imports must cover. Its HE15→19 rise is the evening ramp.
Gas: available / need / spare
Available = gas fleet minus outages. Need = what gas must cover. Spare = the cushion. Amber under 1,500.
Ramp 1h / 3h
Change in gas need from 1 or 3 hours earlier, coloured by percentile for that hour (last 60 days). Green = gas must ramp hard; red = gas backing off.
Headroom
Gas + hydro available − net load. The model's main driver: under ~7,000 it sells.
CAHR
DA forecast as a carbon-adjusted heat rate. 7–8 = efficient gas on the margin; 10–12 = peakers; 12+ = DA already pricing scarcity.
DA fc
Our DA forecast for East ($/MWh).
Read
All-fundamentals model's expected RT − DA. − favours sells, + favours longs. Right 56–70% of the time, but its misses are larger than its hits.
Last 7d / x/7
Average RT − DA in that hour over the last 7 days, and on how many of those days RT beat DA.
Model
The live model's call. Score 5 = tight sell; SW = Spike Watch score (it only buys hours that aren't sells).
