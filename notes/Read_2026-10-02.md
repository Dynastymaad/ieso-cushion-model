- **Load comes in light.** Since July, IESO's forecast has run about 350 MW above actual load, and about 470 MW above at the peak.
- **Wind over-delivers.** Meteologica is above IESO.
- **Capacity keeps returning.** Trips are already negative.
- **The peak is already priced up.** DA is forecast at $75-80 for HE17-20, against a model RT of $63-67. HE19's 7-day bias is -$26.
- **Weak recent results.** Buy-band hours lost money on Sep 21-23 and have been flat since.

## Upside for the longs
- Nuclear is near its 30-day high, and headroom at HE8-10 is only 570-760 MW above the 7,000 MW tight line.

- Scheduled gas outages step up by 1.8 GW on Oct 3. Watch for early starts on that maintenance.
